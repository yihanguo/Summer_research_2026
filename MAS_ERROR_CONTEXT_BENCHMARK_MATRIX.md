# Multi-Agent System Error Context and Benchmark Matrix

This file synthesizes the attached papers, `MAS_LIFE_section_tables.md`, and `BENCHMARKS_HOLISTIC_INTRO.md` into a benchmark-oriented taxonomy for multi-agent system (MAS) research. The column schema follows the screenshot template:

## Notes

- The attached embedding-defense paper is especially useful for rows involving **communication attacks**, **near-benign adversarial messages**, and **topology-dependent signal decay**. It evaluates MMLU, GSM8K, and BBH under Slow Drift, Benign Wrapper, and Chaos Seeding attacks.
- The attached GR-SAP paper is most relevant to **fine-tuning-induced safety degradation** and **alignment-data quality**. It evaluates downstream utility on GSM8K, MATH, HellaSwag, WinoGrande, and MedQA, while measuring Harmful Score on Beavertails, SafeRLHF, WildGuard, and WildJailbreak.
- The attached EVOCHAMBER paper is most relevant to **test-time MAS evolution**, **specialization**, and **cross-agent knowledge transfer**. It uses Hard Math, Hard Code, and AFlow-Stream task streams.
